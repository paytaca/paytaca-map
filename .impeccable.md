# Paytaca Map — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Crypto-curious shoppers and Bitcoin Cash users, primarily in the Philippines but also worldwide, looking for real-world places that accept BCH through Paytaca. Most browse on a phone while out and about, deciding where to eat, shop, or spend. Merchants and the Paytaca community also share the map to show off real adoption. The job to be done: discover a nearby accepting merchant and feel confident and excited to spend BCH there.

### Brand Personality
Playful, celebratory, trustworthy. Voice is warm, upbeat and inviting. The emotional goal is excitement and reward — "your money works here, and look how far it reaches." The interface should feel like a celebration of real-world crypto adoption, not a grim corporate directory.

### Aesthetic Direction
Bright & clean, Paytaca-blue forward with restrained tropical pops. Faint light-blue backgrounds (matching paytaca.com), crisp white cards with soft blue-tinted shadows, generous rounded geometry, bouncy micro-interactions. Paytaca blue is the dominant identity color (matching paytaca.com and the wallet); accent hues (coral, mango, sky, pink) add celebratory energy without competing with the brand. Type uses Rubik throughout (matching the Paytaca app), a clean modern sans that keeps the brand consistent and a touch more formal.

Anti-references (must NOT look like): the old gloomy slate-gray dashboard, flat corporate gray, purple→blue "AI" gradients, pure gray neutrals, glassmorphism everywhere, identical identical card grids.

### Design Principles
1. **Blue-tinted neutrals** — neutrals lean cool slate to sit with the brand blue; no pure gray, no pure black/white.
2. **Blue leads, tropics accent** — Paytaca blue owns primary actions and identity (~60%); coral/mango/sky/pink are purposeful accents (~30/10%), never random.
   Exception: map markers and merchant clusters stay BCH green (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.7)`, stroke `#16a34a`) — green there signals "BCH accepted", not brand identity.
3. **Rounded & bouncy** — large radii, friendly type, micro-interactions (hover lift, gentle pulses) that reward exploration.
4. **Celebrate discovery** — merchant counts and cards read as finds worth celebrating, not rows in a table.
5. **Accessible color** — WCAG AA contrast for text, color never used as the only signal, verify red/green states.
